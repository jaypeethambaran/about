Any column layout works: the explorer detects the budget amount, vendor, dates and so on, and you can adjust the mapping.

Your files stay on this computer. They are read by your browser and never uploaded.

  • Comma, semicolon or tab separated; UTF-8; a title row above the header is fine.
  • Numbers like 1,234.50, 1.234,50 or $1,234; dates like 2027-03-31, 31/03/2027 or 31.03.2027.
  • Optional columns light up extra views: prior-year amount, renewal/end date, notice period, owner, cost centre, monthly columns (Jan…Dec).
  • Excel files: use File → Save As → CSV UTF-8 first.
